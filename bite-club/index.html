Rule 1 of 3 · The transmission cycle

Know the chain

A mosquito doesn't make viruses. It carries them from one infected person to the next. Every link in that chain takes time, and each one is a place where the chain can break.

    Rule 2 of 3 · Prevention

    Break it early

    Aedes females lay their eggs on the inside walls of containers, just above the water line. The eggs become adult mosquitoes in 7 to 10 days. Take away the water and you take away the next generation.

    Rule 3 of 3 · Diagnosis

    Read the case

    These viruses cause similar fevers, so doctors use clues: where the patient has been, what hurts, and how many days they've been sick. The day matters most for testing. Early on you look for the virus itself. Later, you look for the antibodies the body made against it.
