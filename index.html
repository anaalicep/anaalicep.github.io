I'm a PhD student in Biomedical & Biological Sciences at Cornell University, where I work in the Kroon Campos Lab. My research investigates how epitranscriptomic mRNA marks shape arbovirus replication and host–virus interactions across human and mosquito hosts.

Before Cornell, I earned my DVM (Doctor of Veterinary Medicine) at the Federal University of Viçosa (UFV) in Brazil, graduating 2nd in a class of 60. My research background spans veterinary virology, immunology, and antibody biology, with prior work on canine parvovirus, canine distemper virus, and germinal center B cell dynamics.
